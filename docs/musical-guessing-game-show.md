# Musical Whits: The Musical Guessing Game Show

A fast, party-friendly game show where contestants race to identify songs, artists, and musical trivia. Designed for 2 to 4 teams (or individual players), one host, and about 45 to 60 minutes of play.

---

## At a Glance

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Players | 2 to 4 teams of 1 to 4 people |
| Host | 1 (runs music, reads clues, keeps score) |
| Length | 45 to 60 minutes |
| Rounds | 5 plus a Final Showdown |
| Equipment | Speaker, music player or playlist, buzzers (or bells / hand slaps), scoreboard |
| Winner | Highest score after the Final Showdown |

---

## Setup

1. **Build the playlists.** Prepare one playlist per round (see each round for how many tracks). Mix decades and genres so no single team has an edge.
2. **Prep the answer sheet.** For every track, write down the song title, artist, year, and one bonus fact.
3. **Set up buzzers.** Each team needs a way to signal first. A buzzer app, a bell, or slapping the table all work.
4. **Scoreboard.** A whiteboard, a shared screen, or a notebook the host reads aloud after each round.
5. **Pick team names.** Music puns are encouraged.

---

## General Rules

- **Buzz before you answer.** Answers shouted without buzzing do not count.
- **One answer per buzz.** If a team buzzes and gets it wrong, the clip continues and the other teams may buzz in.
- **Wrong-answer lockout.** A team that answers incorrectly cannot buzz again on the same track.
- **Close enough counts.** The host may accept minor mispronunciations or a near-exact title. The host's ruling is final.
- **No phones or lookup apps** during play.
- **Ties** within a round are settled by whoever buzzed first.

---

## Round 1: Name That Tune

Classic warm-up. The host plays a clip and teams race to name the song.

- **Tracks:** 10
- **Clip length:** up to 15 seconds from the start of the song
- **Scoring:**
  - Correct title: **+10**
  - Correct artist as well: **+5 bonus**
  - Wrong answer: **0** (no penalty in the warm-up)

---

## Round 2: Intro Sprint

How fast can you recognize a song from its very first notes?

- **Tracks:** 8
- **Clip length:** plays in stages of 1 second, 3 seconds, then 5 seconds
- **Scoring (correct title):**
  - After 1 second: **+30**
  - After 3 seconds: **+20**
  - After 5 seconds: **+10**
  - Wrong answer: **-5**

---

## Round 3: Lyric Gap

The host plays a clip and stops the music right before a lyric. Teams must sing or say the next line.

- **Tracks:** 8
- **Scoring:**
  - Exact next line: **+20**
  - Mostly right (gist plus key words): **+10**
  - Wrong: **0**
- **Host tip:** pick songs with famous, unambiguous lines.

---

## Round 4: Musical Trivia Lightning

Rapid-fire questions about music, no audio required. Every team writes answers down at the same time, so there is no buzzing.

- **Questions:** 10, about 15 seconds each
- **Example categories:** one-hit wonders, band members, album covers, debut years, movie soundtracks, instruments
- **Scoring:** **+10** per correct answer

**Sample questions**

1. Which band released the album *Abbey Road*?
2. What instrument has 88 keys?
3. Which artist is known as the "Queen of Pop"?
4. Bohemian Rhapsody was released in which decade?
5. What do you call a group of eight musicians?

---

## Round 5: Mashup Mayhem

The host plays two songs layered together (or one song played backwards, or slowed down). Teams name both songs.

- **Tracks:** 6
- **Scoring:**
  - One song correct: **+15**
  - Both songs correct: **+40** (instead of 30)
  - Steal: if a team gets only one, other teams can buzz to name the second song for **+15**

---

## Final Showdown: Wager Round

Each team secretly wagers any part of its current score before hearing the final track.

1. The host announces the category (for example, "90s Movie Soundtracks").
2. Teams write down their wager.
3. The host plays a single 10-second clip.
4. Teams write the song title **and** artist.
5. Both correct: win the wager. Anything wrong: lose the wager.

Highest score after the Final Showdown wins.

---

## Scoring Summary

| Round | Max per track/question | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Name That Tune | 15 | None |
| 2. Intro Sprint | 30 | -5 |
| 3. Lyric Gap | 20 | None |
| 4. Trivia Lightning | 10 | None |
| 5. Mashup Mayhem | 40 | None |
| Final Showdown | Wager | Lose wager |

---

## Host Script (Optional)

> **Opening:** "Welcome to *Musical Whits*, the show where your ears do the thinking! Tonight, our teams will battle through five rounds of tunes, lyrics, and trivia. Fingers on buzzers... let's play!"

> **Between rounds:** Read the scoreboard aloud, then tease the next round with its name and one rule.

> **Before the Final:** "It all comes down to this. One song. One wager. Bet wisely."

> **Closing:** "Congratulations to our champions! Thanks for playing *Musical Whits*!"

---

## Variations

- **Themed nights:** limit every playlist to one decade, genre, or artist.
- **Kids' edition:** use cartoon and movie songs, drop the negative scoring.
- **Solo mode:** each player keeps their own score; no teams.
- **Hum It:** contestants hum a song for their own team to guess. **+15** per correct guess within 30 seconds.
- **Online play:** run it over a video call and use the chat box as the buzzer (first message wins).
